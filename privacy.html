The short version

Prompette collects nothing and sends nothing to any server. There is no account and no analytics.

What Prompette uses

Your microphone, only in voice and follow modes. Voice mode measures how loud you are, to scroll while you speak. Follow mode also turns speech into words to know where you are, using speech recognition that runs on your Mac; Prompette never uses a server for it. The sound is not recorded or kept.

Your local network, only for the iPhone remote, when you turn it on. The Mac and iPhone talk directly over your own network, after you pair them with a four-digit code. Nothing goes over the internet.

What is stored, and where

Your scripts, their rehearsal times and your settings, inside the app's own container on your Mac or iPhone. A script you send to the iPhone is stored there too. Deleting the app removes them.
