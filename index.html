Three intelligent ways to scroll

Whether you follow a strict timing or speak naturally, Prompette adapts to you.

  • 1. Constant Speed. Set your target words-per-minute (WPM). Smooth start, gentle deceleration, with full keyboard & scroll-wheel control whenever you need to adjust.
  • 2. Voice-Paced. The prompter rolls only while you speak and halts automatically when you take a breath. It adapts to your natural speaking rhythm.
  • 3. Word-by-Word Follow. On-device AI speech recognition tracks what you say word by word. Ad-lib freely — Prompette keeps your exact place without missing a beat.
  • Companion Remote. Start, pause, change speed and jump to the next cue marker from an iPhone, iPad or Android device over your own local network, or use it as a pocket prompter.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later; the iPhone and iPad app requires iOS 17 or later, and the Android app Android 8.0 or later. One purchase covers the Mac, iPhone and iPad; the Android app is a separate purchase on Google Play.

Privacy in one paragraph

Prompette has no account and no tracking, and never sends your scripts or your voice anywhere. The microphone is used only on the Mac, in the voice and follow modes you choose, and speech is recognised on your Mac. The remote on your phone or tablet talks to your Mac over your own local network only. The full privacy policy says the same at more length.
